01 / WHO WE ARE

Why UNMTA
was started.

UNMTA was founded to bridge academic theory and practical industry demands. It gives students a collaborative environment to share ideas, develop technical skills and work together on real-world engineering, technology, physics and astrophysics challenges.

Members connect with continuing students, alumni, professionals and industry partners through mentorship, technical activities, career discussions and practical learning.

2.0 VISION, MISSION, AND SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

2.1 VISION

To provide an interactive forum for members to innovate, develop positive attitude, competence and assertiveness in Microprocessor Technology Instrumentation professions, as well as promote career growth in these and related fields.

2.2 MISSION

To bring together all students pursuing Microprocessor Technology and Instrumentation and related disciplines at the University of Nairobi to a common platform where appropriate attitudes can be nurtured as a step towards launching a fruitful career in Microprocessor Technology and instrumentation and achieving excellence in the profession.

2.3 SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
  1. 2.3.1To facilitate members educational and career advancement.
  2. 2.3.2To foster an attitude of teamwork among members.
  3. 2.3.3To promote social welfare of members.
  4. 2.3.4To establish linkages with relevant partner association and organizations.
  5. 2.3.5To facilitate provision of community service by members.
  6. 2.3.6To enhance advocacy on issues of space and development.
  7. 2.3.7To promote a positive attitude among members toward the course.
  8. 2.3.8To popularize Microprocessor Technology and Instrumentation at university in Kenya and all over the world.
  9. 2.3.9To bridge the university-industry gap especially in Microprocessor Technology, physics and related disciplines.

3.0 STRATEGIES OF ACHIEVING THE OBJECTIVES

  • Maintaining a consistence and cohesive membership.
  • Involving members in social and voluntary activities e.g. star gazing, public presentations, university exhibitions, school outreach etc.
  • Organizing interactive sessions with people and organizations in the same discipline and other related disciplines.
  • Establishing a bi-annual Microprocessor Technology and Instrumentation student magazine.
  • Organizing national Microprocessor Technology and Instrumentation campaigns.

4.0 CORE VALUES

Integrity:

We aspire to live to the highest standards of personal honesty and behavior; we never compromise our reputation and always act in the best interests of our members.

Creativity and Innovation:

We are open to new ideas, embrace change, and take disciplined risks to develop sustainable programs.

Collaboration:

We respect and value each other, thrive on our diversity, and work with all to leverage our strength in making a difference for the members and the society.

Ambition:

We are demanding of ourselves, set high goals and are committed to improving the quality of everything we do.

Accountability:

We take personal responsibility for using our resources efficiently, achieving measurable results, and being accountable to members, partners and supporters.
