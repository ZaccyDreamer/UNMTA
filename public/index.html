01 / WHO WE ARE

A place to turn
curiosity into possibility.

UNMTA is the University of Nairobi Microprocessor Technology Association: a student-powered community for people who want to understand how things work—and make something useful with what they learn.

Across microprocessor technology, control and instrumentation, astronomy, astrophysics, physics, hardware and software, we make room for peer learning, practical exploration and meaningful connections.
